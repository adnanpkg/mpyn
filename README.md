## what is multiply.

multiply. connects **local Instagram creators** and **local businesses** in the same city — no agencies, no cold DMs, no working for exposure. every gig, every deal, every payment lives in one place, hyperlocally.

---

## features

| feature | what it does |
|---|---|
| **hyperlocal discovery** | auto-detects your city on signup. search creators if you're a business, search businesses if you're a creator. scoped to your city only. |
| **real-time chat & offers** | chat before committing. businesses send rupee offers, creators accept or reject instantly. |
| **in-app payments** | pay and get paid via UPI, cards, netbanking — all INR, India-only via Razorpay. |
| **ratings & reputation** | every completed gig builds your profile. public ratings, order count, verified history. |
| **public profiles** | visit any creator or business profile. see their work, ratings, and gig history. |
| **pro plan** | zero platform fee, priority visibility, pro badge. pay once monthly, keep everything. |
| **direct support** | dm the founder directly on instagram. no tickets, no bots. |

---

## how it works

```
sign up → auto-detect city → create profile
       ↓
discover creators / businesses in your city
       ↓
chat → negotiate → send offer → accept or reject
       ↓
pay in-app → complete gig → rate each other
```
